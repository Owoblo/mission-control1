QUOTE 02 · FURNITURE

Detailed scope of work

1
Inventory walkthrough and authorization

Walk every applicable room with the College's representative. Mark each item “move,” “leave,” “dispose” or “other destination.” Record the room, description, quantity, approximate size, disassembly needs, unusual handling and destination storage zone. Photograph each included room and attach the approved inventory to the final work order.

2
Area-based preparation

Organize the source operation by Areas A, B and C. Label items by source room and sequence. Protect the agreed travel route and stage completed pieces near the loading path without obstructing required building access.

3
Disassembly and component control

Disassemble only furniture that requires it for safe transport or efficient storage. Bag and label hardware, keep matching components together and retain visible asset labels. Smaller items that safely fit the route may remain assembled to reduce labour and future reassembly risk.

4
Continuous-flow transport

Use source personnel to prepare and stage, truck personnel to maintain shuttles, and destination personnel to control elevator unloading. A working elevator marshal/supervisor will coordinate loading flow and prevent congestion.

5
Fourth-floor storage placement

Place furniture into College-approved zones for desks/tabletops, frames/legs, classroom chairs, office chairs, filing cabinets, cubicle panels, tables and miscellaneous hardware. Keep labels visible, heavy items at safe floor level and access aisles open.

Assumptions and exclusions

Assumptions

  • Two consecutive 8-hour working days
  • Two 26-foot trucks can use the approved loading area
  • Source loading access and destination elevator are reserved
  • Fourth-floor storage is empty, accessible and ready
  • One College representative can make placement decisions
  • Furniture contents are removed before arrival

Not included

  • Disposal or recycling unless inventoried and priced
  • Books, files or loose contents inside furniture
  • Hazardous materials or confidential-document destruction
  • Specialty rigging, safes or unusually heavy equipment
  • Off-site storage or additional destinations
  • Reassembly in storage unless specifically approved

Commercial terms

  • No deposit is assumed. Approved work will be invoiced under the College's authorized commercial payment or purchase-order process.
