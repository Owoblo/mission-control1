Project understanding

Saturn Star Movers will identify, protect, disassemble, transport and reinstall the physical components of the existing computer lab. The supplied plan identifies approximately 50 stations in R1002 and approximately 42 station positions in A2150. This quotation assumes 42 stations are installed in A2150 and up to eight surplus stations are placed in an accessible South Campus storage location designated before work begins.

Scope confirmation required: The final work order must identify the destination of the eight surplus stations and confirm whether a station includes the desk, chair, computer, monitor, keyboard, mouse, cables and any specialty accessories.

Detailed scope of work

1
Pre-move coordination and station control

Complete a site walkthrough; confirm the station count, access route, elevator booking and destination readiness. Assign identifiers R1002-01 through R1002-50 and correlate stations 01–42 with the destination layout. Photograph representative cable arrangements and identify dual-monitor or specialty stations.

2
Protection, labelling and preparation

Protect floors, doorways, elevator interiors and corridor corners as required. Apply matching labels to each desk, chair, computer, monitor, peripheral set and cable bag. Bag and retain standard fasteners with their furniture components.

3
Disassembly and equipment handling

Remove monitors and computer towers before furniture disassembly. Break down desks or benching systems into numbered sections as reasonably required for safe transport. Wrap furniture and handle electronics separately from frames and heavier components.

4
Transportation

Load electronics in protected zones and secure furniture components for transport. Operate two 26-foot trucks to reduce handling congestion and keep the workstation sequence controlled between campuses.

5
Installation and placement

Assemble furniture in A2150 according to the approved destination plan. Place numbered computers, monitors, chairs and cable kits at their assigned stations. Place surplus stations in the college-designated location and complete a count with the authorized representative.

Included equipment and protections

  • Commercial carts, panel carts and dollies
  • Moving blankets and standard protective wrap
  • Floor runners and corner protection as required
  • Labels, hardware bags and cable bags
  • Standard furniture installation tools
  • Basic monitor/electronics padding
  • Loading, transport and final placement
  • Working project supervisor

Responsibilities

Saturn Star Movers

  • Physical packing, handling and placement
  • Furniture disassembly and reassembly
  • Station-number control and hardware retention
  • Daily site housekeeping and final count

St. Clair College

  • IT shutdown, backup and safe disconnect

  • Exclusive elevator and loading access

IT boundary: This proposal covers physical equipment handling. Network, electrical, data-cabling, software configuration and technical system testing remain the College's responsibility unless added by written change order.

Assumptions and conditions

Pricing assumptions

  • Six-person crew, including a working supervisor.
  • Two 26-foot trucks for two consecutive 8-hour working days.
  • Both campuses are available during agreed work hours.
  • A2150 is vacant, construction-complete and ready to receive furniture.
  • Main double elevators are reserved for the crew.
  • All items fit safely through confirmed doors and elevators.
  • No hazardous materials or regulated disposal.

Not included

  • IT disconnect/reconnect or computer testing
  • Electrical or network/data work
  • Custom crating or rented specialty monitor crates
  • Storage fees or off-site warehousing
  • Disposal, recycling or asset destruction
  • After-hours premiums, permits or paid parking
  • Work arising from unready rooms or unavailable elevators

Change control

This proposal is based on the supplied drawings and written request. A walkthrough is required before scheduling. If actual quantities, furniture construction, access conditions or technical responsibilities materially differ, Saturn Star will provide a written change description and price before undertaking additional work. At the College's request, approved additional time may be billed at the quoted combined rate of $395.00 per hour plus HST.
