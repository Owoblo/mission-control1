Fidelis Operations Lead SOP

This is the short working SOP for Fidelis as the Waterloo operations lead. The role is designed to run booked jobs, dispatch, truck setup, crew setup, and post-move actuals. It is not a sales or admin control role.

Role: Operations Lead Branch: Waterloo Workspace: Operations

What Fidelis Controls

  • Booked jobs for the Waterloo branch
  • Crew assignment and crew notes
  • Truck reservation status, vendor, size, pickup, return, and reservation details
  • Ops checklist items like access, parking, tools, and job packet readiness
  • Crew hours after the move
  • Actual hours and actual crew after the move
  • Marking jobs complete and triggering the review request flow

What Fidelis Does Not Control

  • Pipeline, inbox, or normal sales workflow
  • Quote building, quote revisions, or discounts
  • Finance, analytics, or rep management
  • Creating users, changing roles, or resetting passwords
  • Changing the truck count sold on the quote

Daily Workflow

  1. Log in and open the Operations page.
  2. Review booked and deposit-paid jobs for Waterloo.
  3. Open Ops Setup on each job.
  4. Assign crew, add crew notes, and confirm truck reservation details.
  5. Confirm access, parking, tools, and job packet readiness.
  6. After the move, log actual hours and actual crew.
  7. Mark the move complete once the job is done.

Guardrails

Truck count is pulled from the quote. Operations works around that plan, but does not change what was sold.
Stay inside the Waterloo branch scope unless ownership explicitly expands it.

Escalate When

  • The customer wants the quote or commercial terms changed
  • The invoice, deposit, or balance logic looks wrong
  • The truck plan sold to the customer looks wrong
  • A new team account or password reset is needed
  • There is a dispute, damage issue, or exception that affects pricing or commitments
