Saturn Star CRM

Saturn Star CRM is the business workspace within Saturn Star OS used by authorized Saturn Star Moving staff to manage moving enquiries, customer relationships, quotes, operations, and business email.

Connecting Google email

Authorized users can connect their own Google Workspace mailbox. With their permission, the CRM identifies the connected email account, reads Gmail messages for business correspondence and reply handling, and sends email from that account. Offline access allows the connection to work when the user is not actively signed in.

Mailbox connection does not by itself enable campaign sending. Access is limited to authorized staff and configured business mailboxes.

Your choice and your data

Google access requires your consent. You can revoke access in your Google Account connections settings. Our Privacy Policy explains data use, storage, sharing, and deletion requests. Our Terms of Use explain permitted use.
