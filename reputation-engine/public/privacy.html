Saturn Star CRM Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy describes how Saturn Star Moving uses information through Saturn Star CRM, the business workspace within Saturn Star OS hosted at go.quote2move.com.

Information we access and collect

The CRM processes information supplied by authorized staff and business contacts, including contact details, moving enquiries, quotes, operational records, and business correspondence. When you authorize Google access, the integration receives account identifiers and an email address, OAuth credentials needed to maintain the connection, and the Gmail data permitted by the scopes you approve. Gmail read access can include message headers, bodies, and attachments. Gmail send access allows messages to be sent from the connected mailbox.

How we use information

We use this information to identify and connect your mailbox, support business correspondence, send authorized messages, handle replies, verify delivery and authentication, and operate and troubleshoot the CRM. Refresh tokens allow continued access without requiring a new sign-in for every operation.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Saturn Star CRM’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access and sharing

Authorized business users may access business records and correspondence as needed for their work. Infrastructure providers process information to host and operate the service, including Vercel for application hosting and Supabase for database storage. Google processes requests made to Gmail. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Mailivery is a separate service. Connecting a mailbox directly to Mailivery involves its own consent and privacy terms; a CRM connection alone does not authorize that separate connection.

Storage and security

Google refresh tokens are encrypted before database storage. The application restricts mailbox connection management to authorized administrators and uses HTTPS for connections. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Connection credentials are retained while needed to support the connection. Business correspondence and CRM records may be retained for ongoing operations, recordkeeping, and applicable legal obligations. To request deletion of your connection credentials or other personal information, contact the address below and identify the relevant account. We will verify the request and explain any information that must be retained. Backup copies may remain until the applicable backup retention cycle ends.

Your controls

You can revoke Saturn Star CRM’s Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized Google access but does not automatically delete previously stored CRM records. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of those records.
