📱 Your App — Crew Calendar

Your job information is in the Crew Calendar. This is where you see your assigned moves, job details, timing, and route.

go.quote2move.com → Log in → Crew Calendar

You'll receive a text message with your login details. Use the password you were given. If you forget it, contact your operations lead.

📋 How to Use the Crew Calendar

1
Log in and check your upcoming jobs

When you log in, you see a list of your assigned moves sorted by date. TODAY and TOMORROW are highlighted so you know what's coming up.

2
Open a job to see the details

Tap any job to see: Customer name, move date, start time, origin and destination address, truck reservation number (if booked), and your crew lead.

3
Read the dispatch brief

The dispatch brief tells you: what you're moving, what is NOT moving (do not load this), access info (parking, elevator, stairs), and any special items (piano, fragile, appliances). Read this before you arrive.

4
Check in with the crew lead

The crew lead has the full job file. They will brief the team on arrival. Follow their direction during the move.

✅ Rules for Every Move

⏰
Arrive on timeThe customer is waiting. If you're running late, call operations right away — not the customer directly.
📋
Read the dispatch brief before touching anythingIt lists what's going, what's staying, and any fragile or high-value items. Knowing this before you start saves problems.
🚫
Do NOT move items on the "Do Not Move" listThe customer confirmed what stays. If they change their mind on the day, the crew lead must call the office before adding anything to the truck.
📸
Document any damage before movingIf an item already has damage (scratch, dent) before you touch it, point it out to the customer and crew lead immediately. Take a photo.
🔒
Never leave the truck unlocked or unattendedEspecially when the truck is loaded. Park in a visible spot and keep it locked.
💬
All customer communication goes through the crew leadMovers should not discuss pricing, complaints, or scheduling changes with the customer. Direct everything to the crew lead.

⚠️ If Something Goes Wrong

Damage during the move: Stop work immediately. Notify the crew lead. Do not move the item further or attempt to repair it. The crew lead contacts operations — operations handles the customer conversation.
Customer asks to add extra items on the day: This may change the estimate. Crew lead must call the office first. We do not add major items without office approval — this is a binding estimate situation.
Running significantly over time: Crew lead calls operations before the estimate window closes. The office manages the conversation about additional charges.
