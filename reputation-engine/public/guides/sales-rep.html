Mission Control Platform · Internal Use Only

Sales Rep

🌅 Starting Your Day

1
Open Morning BriefingGo to /sales/read-first — this shows your priority leads, scheduled follow-ups, and any booked moves for today. Start here every shift.
2
Check the InboxGo to /sales/inbox — any new SMS replies or emails from leads appear here. Respond to anything urgent before making outbound calls.
3
Check your Follow-Up QueueOn the leads page, the "Needs Follow-Up" tab shows every lead with an action due today. Work through this list in priority order.

📞 Handling a New Lead

1
Claim the leadNew leads appear in Inbox → New Leads. Click Claim — this assigns it to you and moves it to your pipeline.
2
Call within 5 minutesSpeed wins moves. Use the browser dialer (green Dialer button, bottom right). Introduce yourself: "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with Saturn Star Moving — you reached out about a move?"
3
Collect the essentialsMoving from / to, move date, type of home, anything special (piano, appliances, packing). Update the lead card as you talk.
4
Send the survey linkClick Send Survey on the lead. Customer gets a text with a link — they upload photos of their home. This is how we quote accurately without visiting.
5
Set a follow-upIf they haven't responded in 2 hours, follow up. Set the follow-up date on the lead so it shows in your queue.
Script: "We already have a system that reviews your home's photos and builds the estimate automatically — no one needs to come out, and the price you see is the price you pay on moving day."

📋 Building and Sending a Quote

1
Scan the surveyOnce photos are submitted, open the lead and click Scan Survey. The AI analyzes the photos and pre-fills the inventory.
2
Review the inventoryIn the quote builder, go through each room. Add anything missing. For appliances (washer, fridge, stove) — confirm with the customer if they're moving them, then click Include Back.
3
RecalculateClick Recalculate to generate crew size, hours, and pricing based on the inventory. Review the numbers — make sure the margin is 65%+.
4
Send the quoteClick Send Quote. Customer gets an SMS with a link to their personalized estimate. They can accept and pay the deposit directly from the link.
5
Follow up on the quoteIf not accepted within 2 hours: "Hi [Name], just checking that you received the estimate — want me to walk you through anything?" The system auto-follows up too, but a personal touch closes more.

✅ Booking a Move

When the customer accepts the quote and pays the deposit:

  • The lead automatically moves to Booked stage
  • Click Confirm Job to lock in the move date and crew plan
  • Operations gets notified automatically
  • You get credit for the booking in your rep leaderboard

💬 Using the Inbox (SMS + Email)

SMS Inbox

  • Shows all two-way texts per lead
  • Reply directly from the thread
  • AI can draft a reply — click Smart Compose

Email Inbox

  • Customer replies to your quote emails land here
  • Reply from inside the CRM — keeps history in one place

📱 Using the Dialer

  1. Click the green Dialer button (bottom-right of any screen)
  2. Enter the phone number or it auto-fills from the lead
  3. Click Call — the call comes from the business number 226-773-2993
  4. All calls are recorded automatically for training and accuracy
  5. After the call, add notes on the lead card
Tip: If a lead goes cold, use the Smart Compose button in the SMS tab — the AI reads the full conversation history and writes the right follow-up message automatically.

🎯 Key Rules

  • Call within 5 minutes of a new lead coming in — speed wins moves
  • Always say "binding estimate" — no surprise fees, what they see is what they pay
  • Save the lead before leaving the page (amber warning shows unsaved changes)
  • Never quote a number without seeing the inventory — always send the survey link first
  • If you can't close over the phone, ask: "Would it help to have someone come by and do a quick walkthrough?"
