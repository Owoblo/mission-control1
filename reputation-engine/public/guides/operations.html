🚛 Before Each Move — Pre-Move Checklist

48 Hours Before

  • Crew assigned and confirmed?
  • U-Haul / truck reserved? (Log reservation # in CRM)
  • Deposit received?
  • Customer confirmed via SMS?
  • Packing materials ordered?
  • Any access issues flagged? (elevator booking, condo rules)

Day Before

  • Text crew lead: job details + start time
  • Confirm truck pickup time
  • Share dispatch brief with crew lead
  • Review any "Do Not Move" items
  • Check for high-value / fragile items needing special handling

📋 Reading a Dispatch Brief

Every booked job has an auto-generated dispatch brief. It includes:

  • Customer: Name, phone, move date, route
  • Crew Plan: Number of movers, trucks, estimated hours
  • Inventory: What is going (by room)
  • Do Not Move: Items the customer confirmed are staying — do not load these
  • Access Notes: Parking, elevator, stairs, long carry
  • Special Handling: Fragile items, piano, appliances
  • Customer Notes: Concerns, things mentioned on the call
The dispatch brief is built from the actual sales conversation. If something seems off or missing, check with the sales rep who handled the lead before the day of the move.

🔑 Truck Reservation (U-Haul)

1
Book the truckLog into the company U-Haul account. Book based on the job's estimated truck count in the dispatch brief.
2
Log the reservation in CRMOpen the job lead → Operations tab → enter the reservation number, truck size, and pickup time. This goes into the dispatch brief automatically.
3
The crew lead will see itThe reservation number appears in the dispatch brief the crew lead receives. They show their license — it charges to the company card.

👥 Crew Assignment

  • Assign crew from the Operations job page → click the job → Crew section
  • Crew lead gets an SMS notification with job details when assigned
  • Movers see their job on the Crew Calendar at /crew/calendar
  • If a crew member cancels, update the job and re-assign immediately

⚠️ Risk Flags — Handle These Immediately

The High-Risk filter surfaces jobs with:

  • Deposit not received — do not dispatch crew until paid
  • No crew assigned — must be filled 24+ hours before
  • No truck booked — must be reserved 48+ hours before
  • Tomorrow's job — double-check everything tonight

📱 Pre-Move Reminder System

The system automatically sends the customer an SMS 7 days before their move date. It also creates a task in the CRM: "Call to confirm — go over inventory, binding terms, and access details." This call is your job to make, not the sales rep's.

After the Move

  • Mark job as Completed in the Operations dashboard
  • Log actual hours and crew (for payout calculations)
  • Flag any damage claims or incidents immediately
  • The review request triggers automatically after completion
