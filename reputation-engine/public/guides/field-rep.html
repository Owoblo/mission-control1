🏠 What Is an In-Home Move Consultation?

This is a premium visit for larger or complex moves where the customer needs human reassurance before trusting the quote. You are not going to figure everything out from scratch — the office has already reviewed the listing photos and built a preliminary move plan. You are going to verify, confirm, and build trust.

You should arrive saying: "Our office already reviewed the listing photos and prepared a preliminary move plan. I'm here to verify the details and make sure the estimate is accurate." — not "So what are you moving?"

📱 Before the Visit — Prepare Your File

1
Open the lead in the CRMFind the lead at go.quote2move.com/sales. The purple banner at the top shows your consultation appointment details.
2
Read the Pre-Visit BriefClick "View Pre-Visit Brief" in the purple banner. This has the customer's preliminary inventory, route, move date, and what the office still needs you to verify.
3
Review listing photosThe lead shows MLS listing photos if the property is listed. Scroll through them before you drive over. Know what you're walking into.
4
Check the preliminary inventoryThe "Inventory Snapshot" in the lead shows items the AI detected from photos. You'll be verifying these on-site, not starting from zero.

🚪 Arrival Script

"Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] with Saturn Star Movers. Our office already reviewed the listing photos and built a preliminary move plan. I'm here to verify everything in person, check anything the photos don't show, and make sure the estimate is completely accurate. This should take about 20–30 minutes."

Then ask permission before recording or photographing:

"Is it okay if I take a few photos or short videos for our estimating team? This only helps the office prepare an accurate estimate and make sure the crew comes properly prepared."

📞 Live Handoff to Office

When the walkthrough is complete, do the live handoff while you're still there.

"I've completed the walkthrough and verified the main details. If it works for you, I'll call our office now, give them the verified inventory, and they can start preparing the written estimate while I'm still here — that way if they have any questions, we can answer them right away."

On the CRM, tap 📞 Request Live Estimate (in the purple banner). This alerts the central team immediately.

What to tell the office:

"I'm here with [Customer Name]. Walkthrough complete. MLS photos were [mostly accurate / needed corrections: explain]. We need to add [e.g. garage items, 40 extra boxes, partial kitchen packing]. Looking like a [1/2]-truck move. Access is [good / has long carry at destination]. Move date is [Date]. Customer is mainly concerned about [damage/timing/price]."

📝 After the Visit — Update the CRM

1
Add consultation notesOn the lead page, click the Consultation tab. Add everything you verified — box count, appliances, access issues, customer concerns.
2
Update the inventoryIn the estimate builder, add items the photos didn't show (garage, basement, extra boxes). Use Include Back for appliances the customer confirmed are moving.
3
Upload photos/videosIf you took photos on-site, upload them via the media upload button on the lead. These help the office finalize the quote.
4
Save the leadClick Save Lead before leaving the page. The amber banner shows if there are unsaved changes.
⚠️ Do not quote a final price on-site unless the office has confirmed the number. You can say "This is looking like a two-truck move" or "The office will have the full estimate to you today" — but do not name a specific dollar amount without office sign-off.
