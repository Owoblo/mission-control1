In-Home Consultation Recording — Full Intelligence Layer

The recording is not just about inventory. It is a full intelligence capture of one of the most important moments in the sales process — the in-person consultation. Hit Record when you walk in. Everything said is captured. When you leave, the AI goes to work across four dimensions:

📦 Inventory & Logistics
Items mentioned room by room, special concerns (stairs, narrow doors, heavy equipment), pricing flags. Pre-fills quote builder automatically.
🧡 Client Care Notes
Everything the client said they were careful about — the antique dresser, the crystal collection, the grandfather clock. Flagged and attached to the lead so the crew knows before move day.
🎯 Sales Coaching
Replay the consultation. Review how the rep handled objections, built rapport, presented price, and closed. Score the call. Identify exactly where deals are won or lost.
📈 Rep Training Library
Best consultations become training material. New reps study real recordings of what great looks like. Weaknesses get corrected fast — not months later after lost jobs.
One-tap record in browser Full Whisper transcription Client care items flagged Crew briefing auto-generated Sales style scoring Objection handling review Rep performance tracking Training clip library Replay at any time Pre-fills inventory + quote
The big picture: Right now knowledge lives in John's head. When a rep does a consultation, what they notice — what the client said they cared about, how the conversation really went — mostly disappears. This feature captures all of it. The client who said "please be careful with my mother's china cabinet" — that note is waiting for the crew when they show up. The rep who talked too much about price too early — John can coach that exact moment. This is how a moving company becomes a brand people trust and refer.
