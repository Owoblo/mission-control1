# Follow-up radar operating procedure

Open `/sales/follow-up-radar` at 9am and 4pm Toronto. These are queue review times, not instructions to contact every customer twice daily.

1. Review new enquiries, new inbound messages, missed callbacks and near-term moves first. Read the latest SMS, email, call notes and transcript, not just the sales stage. A completed call log does not prove a customer conversation; check voicemail and outcome.
2. Give every genuine opportunity one responsible owner, an evidenced category, a concrete next action and a next review date. Record the exact callback time and channel in the action. Do not infer a firm move date from an estimate or placeholder.
3. Make the appropriate contact: call, leave a useful voicemail when appropriate, then a short contextual text; email where useful or requested. Respect opt-outs, channel limitations, customer timing and other representatives' recent work. Do not send the same message across every channel automatically.
4. After each attempt or conversation, log the result and save the next action/date. Mark a definitive outcome only with evidence. No response means unresolved, not a confirmed loss. If there is no response after reasonable spaced attempts, set a later internal review rather than chasing indefinitely.
5. A task is finished when the plan moves forward or an evidenced outcome closes it. Checking off a task while its plan is still overdue will cause it to reopen on the next check.

## Categories and timing

| Category | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting on house sale / closing | Record the actual sale condition or expected closing; agree a useful check-in. |
| Waiting on date / storage / family | Record the dependency and check again at the agreed time. |
| Waiting on inventory / addresses | Request only the missing details needed to price or book. |
| Considering quote | Address the last concern and agree the next decision step. |
| Price or scope concern | Clarify scope and feasible options; no unauthorized price promise. |
| Promised callback | Preserve the customer's day, time and channel. |
| Waiting on client introduction | Keep the referrer relationship; create a customer job when enough information exists. |
| Customer will contact us | Internal review only; do not turn a reminder into permission to chase. |
| Future move | Review near the relevant decision window. |
| Past enquiry — outcome unknown | Verify internally before assuming the move remains available. |
| Needs conversation review | Review the evidence and qualify; distinguish customers from suppliers, tests and duplicates. |
| Confirmed outcome | Record what happened and why the plan is closed. |

Managers review overdue tasks, unassigned enquiries and partnership handoff exceptions at both queue reviews. Confirm the receiving sales rep owns each customer job; retain the partner relationship separately. Meetups remain with John unless assigned otherwise.

## System behaviour and limits

The radar reads current sales leads and partner opportunities. New eligible sales records automatically enter a triage queue. Existing reviewed leads become due on their review date; newer inbound timestamps or call logs reopen review. The task reconciler runs every 15 minutes and maintains stable tasks without sending customers messages. Partnership exceptions are summarized in a manager task.

Reviewed plans suppress generic automated quote, survey, reactivation and lost-feedback chases. Inbound responses and booked appointment/move reminders retain their existing controls. The radar does not invent conversation summaries or replace checking the full conversation. Email activity depends on the CRM recording it; the radar is not an independent mailbox monitor. Categories do not rewrite stage, booking, owner assignment or historical conversations.

The initial October 20 review used 60 days of communications. It is a starting review, not a claim that every historical CRM record has been qualified. Unclassified records remain visible for triage.

## Recorded closures and exhausted contact attempts

A documented closure ends the follow-up plan in the same save. Representatives can choose **Contact Attempts Exhausted — End Outreach** and record the attempted contacts. This is an operational closure, not a claim that the customer chose another mover. Ordinary timing uncertainty or a still-expected reply remains in Nurture.

Closed plans do not return just because time passes, a representative attempts another outbound call, or an old record is updated. New inbound messages or completed inbound calls can surface an internal review without changing the sales stage or authorizing an automatic chase. A newer recorded loss supersedes an older active follow-up plan. Missing transcripts do not prove a rep failed to follow up: reconcile rep notes and the actual communication history before creating work.
