# Saturn Star CRM Desk Reference

Keep this beside the CRM during live work.

Version: 1.0  
Effective date: July 25, 2026  
Owner: Mission Control operations

## The call flow

1. Connect: acknowledge the person and their situation.
2. Capture: name, phone, email, branch.
3. Context: date, closing/possession timing, origin, destination, goal.
4. Evidence: stored listing scan, photos, video, in-home, or verbal inventory.
5. Plan: Prepare → Move out → Hold → Move in → Settle.
6. Confirm: inventory, meaningful access risks, services, constraints.
7. Price: route, crew, truck, hours, direct costs, margin.
8. Next step: confirmation, quote, tentative hold, follow-up, deposit, or manager.

## Ask one question at a time

Good:

> “What part is already firm—the move-out date, the new home, or neither yet?”

Bad:

> “How many boxes, stairs, elevators, parking, packing, disassembly, fragile items, oversized items, dates, keys, and stops?”

## Evidence path

- Stored listing match: scan, review, send room list for confirmation.
- No listing: request photos.
- Complex/hidden context: video survey.
- High-risk or commercial: in-home consultation.
- Small/simple: verbal inventory with known dimensions.

## Do not send a final price when

- route is wrong or incomplete;
- inventory has zero/unknown volume;
- customer has not confirmed material scope;
- a storage or extra stop is missing;
- specialty work is unpriced;
- destination is unknown and later delivery is being presented as certain;
- the service/margin check is blocked.

## Edge-case routing

- Two pickups → one destination: conjoint move.
- Multiple operational stops: quote legs.
- House → storage → future house: move-out, hold, move-in phases.
- Customer container/truck: scoped job price or labour-only package; confirm fill, density, contents, access, and placement.
- Packing only: one customer package with internal labour and material assumptions.
- Junk: separate service/leg + disposal.
- Cleaning: separate service with square footage/condition.
- Unknown date: flexible date + follow-up/tentative reservation.
- Unknown destination: quote known work; keep delivery provisional.

## Fixed-price white-glove

- Sell the confirmed outcome as one clear job price; keep crew hours, trucks, materials, access, risk, and margin visible internally.
- Absorb ordinary variation that stays within the verified scope and capacity band.
- Revise for material changes such as added rooms, major volume, specialty items, extra stops, packing, access, or route changes.
- If items are removed, update and recalculate. Reduce only when the required resources or cost band genuinely changes.
- For a 20-foot container, confirm fill level, density, contents evidence, loose versus boxed goods, access, placement/assembly, travel, and whether Saturn Star supplies transportation.
- “All boxes included” means standard boxes and ordinary packing materials reasonably required for the verified household scope—not specialty crates, extraordinary/hazardous volume, unused take-home stock, or later scope additions.

Say:

> “We price the complete move you described. Let us know what changes; if it changes the resources required, we will revise the price fairly.”

## Uncertain but quoteable

- Exact date unknown: record the best window (“first week of August”) and why.
- House not sold: lock the known inventory/services; schedule a sale-status check-in.
- Destination city only: model travel to the city; ask house/apartment/condo/storage.
- Home not chosen: keep final mileage and access provisional.
- Storage end unknown: use a written allowance and nudge for the release date.
- Item may be sold/donated: mark **decision pending**, include it in safe capacity planning or show a removable allowance, and set a decision follow-up. Recalculate before promising a price reduction.
- Never restart the plan when details change—adjust only the affected pieces.

Use one of three labels:

- **Firm:** date, route, scope, and material risks confirmed.
- **Locked scope:** known work priced; variable pieces identified.
- **Planning:** useful range with assumptions and a dated next milestone.

Say:

> “We can map and price what you know now, then tighten the date or destination with you when it is confirmed.”

If anything changes after sending or payment, create a revision. Never overwrite history.

## Tentative reservation

Use for reviewing estimate, comparing, waiting for sale/closing, partner decision, or uncertain date.

Always set:

- reason;
- decision date;
- unresolved notes;
- optional customer SMS.

It is a courtesy hold—not a booking or deposit.

## Before sending

- Open the **Estimate Readiness Workspace**
- Evidence: MLS, photos, video, list, rep intake, or customer confirmation visible
- Inventory: moving, staying, and decision-pending items reconciled
- No included item with unknown cubic feet
- Customer confirmed the proposed fixed-price scope
- Correct customer and branch
- Correct full addresses
- Date or explicit flexible-date assumption
- Origin and destination access checked separately
- Garage, basement, storage, closets, shed, attic, patio, and boxes asked where relevant
- Services visible
- Crew/truck plausible
- Price and margin safe
- Customer-facing explanation clean
- Deposit/billing model correct
- No internal notes exposed

The four gates are:

1. Evidence collected
2. Inventory reconciled
3. Route and access
4. Commercial promise

Do not let MLS, AI, photos, or a phone list act as the complete source of truth by itself. Combine the evidence and use the customer’s latest confirmed decision.

## Before booking

- Quote accepted
- Correct payment account
- Deposit recorded
- Tentative hold converted
- Operations handoff initialized

## Before move day

- Customer details
- Route and stops
- Access/parking exceptions
- Crew assigned and confirmed
- Truck plan reserved
- Equipment ready
- Job packet ready
- Storage/key/elevator timing confirmed

## After move

- Actual hours and crew
- Scope changes/issues
- Payment reconciliation
- Review request
- Referral/partnership context
- Estimate-versus-actual learning

## Loss reasons

Use the truth: price, timing, availability, competitor, service scope, experience, trust, no response, not a fit, cancelled, or DIY.

Write facts, not insults or speculation.

## Escalate

- Low margin or unusual price override
- Payment/Stripe mismatch
- Duplicate accepted quote/payment
- Specialty or unsafe scope
- Damage/dispute
- Media count mismatch
- AI/customer contradiction
- Wrong branch or identity
- System state does not make sense

## Never

- Ask fifteen questions at once.
- Claim a scan source incorrectly.
- Treat zero volume as a real estimate.
- Send duplicates because the page is slow.
- Promise expired availability.
- Change an accepted quote silently.
- Let a promise exist without a date.

## Controlled-copy rule

Mission Control is the source of truth when the software and this reference differ. Report the mismatch to a manager or the document owner, use the safest supported workflow, and revise this reference when the operating change is approved.
