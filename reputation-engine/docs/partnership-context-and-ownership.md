# Shared partnership and sales context

October 9, 2026. These rules apply to Partnerships and Sales together.

## Ownership and callbacks

An accepted, assigned Sales handoff is Sales nurture, not an unanswered Partnerships task. It stays visible in the inbox under Waiting and in Sales Opportunities. Acceptance is not proof that a callback happened. Keep the owner, next action and due date visible. If any active handoff explicitly returns to Partnerships, Partnerships must review it again. Completed handoffs do not hide new enquiries. Respect “I will reach out” instructions; do not generate repeated nudges.

## Read the relationship and the job together

Open **Shared Partnerships & Sales context** in the partnership conversation or the Sales handoff panel. It brings together the partnership thread, matching Sales SMS, linked client email, call activity and available recordings/transcripts. Read the labeled realtor/client context before contacting either person. One realtor can introduce several different clients; keep those jobs separate. A realtor can also have their own move.

Link jobs through the selected partner/referral record. Shared phone numbers are ambiguous: unlinked phone history must not be automatically merged. Duplicate storage of one provider message is shown once; separate messages with identical wording remain separate. Missing history is displayed as incomplete. Suggested replies require complete, unambiguous context and stop when Sales owns follow-up or a later reply already exists. This does not lock out deliberate manual messages or guarantee concurrent reps cannot send simultaneously.

## Response reporting

The **Response & referral report** in Partnership replies covers the last 24 hours or 14 days across permitted markets. Count people by normalized phone/contact identity, not number of texts or identical names. Show substantive replies, reaction-only responses, automatic-only responses and opt-outs separately. A “like” is engagement, not a sales opportunity. Opt-outs may overlap an earlier substantive response. Reported periods include responses to older outreach; do not divide those counts by today's sends and call it a batch response rate.

## Sending windows and recovery

Cold SMS execution checks the campaign's local timezone and start/end hours, immediately before provider submission as well as at job processing. Default: Toronto 10:00–17:00 when no window is configured. Decimal closing hours are supported (17.5 means 17:30). Delayed jobs are held as failed with a window reason after closing or on a later day, rather than spilling into the evening. Releasing them requires a reviewed new schedule. Provider-accepted jobs may reconcile their receipts after closing without sending again. Explicit scheduled conversation replies retain their own schedule.

The sequence worker runs once per minute, keeps the existing bounded batch limit, and claims jobs only when ready to process them. It stops starting new jobs after 40 seconds of the 60-second invocation. Configured capacity is a ceiling, not a delivery promise: provider latency, receipt delays and failures still affect completion. A requested target means confirmed delivered; queued, accepted and awaiting final status are different figures.

Only consider a second number after a confirmed technical failure or reconciled never-sent first attempt. Verify identity, number ownership, mobile eligibility, suppression and previous outreach across channels. Do not retry a pending/accepted attempt or use another number to bypass an opt-out. Retain the primary number and full attempt history. No automatic second-number sender is installed by this change.

## Referral outcomes

Show referral-channel records separately from customer moving opportunities. Count each linked lead once; a partner-role handoff is not another booked customer job. Quoted means currently quoted; booked includes later operational stages; completed is a subset of booked. These are current CRM stages, not an audited revenue or historical conversion report. Link the actual customer's job to the referring realtor so its booking is visible alongside the relationship.

## Owner-approved reply rules

- Use **John** only. Do not introduce him by full name. Existing relationships usually need no repeated introduction.
- Price depends on inventory, route, floors/access and packing. We avoid hourly pricing and provide a flat binding estimate after reviewing scope. Ask what needs moving and only the missing details. Clarify own move versus client when unclear. Do not invent a price or availability; preserve existing agreed quotes.
- Coverage includes the GTA, Orangeville, Vaughan and Barrie. Other locations require confirmed coverage before promising service.
- Moving is the primary service; move-out cleans are also offered. Clarify a cleaning-only enquiry rather than assuming a move.
- Approved Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/saturnstarmovers/.
- Review the shared conversation before resending a business card, asking for an address already given or promising a callback another rep has completed.
