SUPPORTING LENSES

The same spine, viewed for a specific decision

These are overlays—not separate competing models.

People & permissions

Who may view, mutate, approve, dispatch, refund, or close each state—and at company, branch, or owned-record scope.

Communications & automation

Calls, SMS, email and forms attach evidence to the spine. Automation schedules bounded actions and yields on replies or gates.

Data ownership

Supabase preserves operational truth; media storage preserves evidence; derived views explain readiness, risk and next action.

Partnerships & attribution

Partner → referral → person → quote → job → outcome is one traceable source relationship, not a parallel lifecycle.

Security & trust

Sessions, public tokens and provider signatures enter through different boundaries, then converge on validation, policy and audit.

AUTHORITY CONTRACT

Rules that keep the model honest

State: the job spine owns operational lifecycle state.
Orchestration: Mission Control turns state into owned work.
Evidence: channels and providers report facts; they do not own lifecycle state.
Automation: may act only inside explicit policy and handoff boundaries.
Money: internal payment state changes only after reconciliation.
Readiness: critical blockers prevent dispatch and execution.
Overrides: require authority, reason, actor, time and evidence.
Exceptions: must name consequence, owner and next action.
