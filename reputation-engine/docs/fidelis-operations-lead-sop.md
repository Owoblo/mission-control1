# Fidelis Operations Lead SOP

## Role

Fidelis is the `operations_lead` for the `waterloo` branch.

This role is meant to run branch operations after the move is sold. It is not a sales role and it is not an admin role.

## Core Responsibilities

- Review booked and deposit-paid jobs for the Waterloo branch.
- Assign crew members to each booked move.
- Add dispatch notes for the crew.
- Manage truck reservation details around the truck plan already sold on the quote.
- Confirm readiness items like access, parking, tools, and job packet.
- Log crew hours after the move.
- Save actual crew / actual hours after the move.
- Mark moves complete and trigger review-request follow-up.

## What Fidelis Can Control

- `assignedCrew`
- `crewNote`
- `crewHours`
- `truckReservationStatus`
- `truckVendor`
- `truckSize`
- `truckPickupLocation`
- `truckPickupTime`
- `truckReturnLocation`
- `truckReservationNumber`
- `truckReservationNotes`
- `opsChecklist`

## What Fidelis Cannot Control

- Sales pipeline
- Inbox
- Quote building or quote revisions
- Finance
- Analytics
- Rep management
- User creation, deletion, role changes, or password changes

## Important Guardrails

- Truck count comes from the accepted quote. Ops does not change the quoted truck count.
- Fidelis should work only inside the Waterloo branch unless ownership explicitly changes scope.
- If pricing, discounts, invoices, quote terms, or customer sales commitments need to change, escalate to owner or manager.

## Daily Workflow

1. Open the Operations page.
2. Review upcoming booked jobs for Waterloo.
3. Open `Ops Setup` on each job.
4. Assign crew and add crew notes.
5. Update truck reservation status and details.
6. Confirm access, parking, tools, and job packet.
7. On or after move day, log actual hours and actual crew.
8. Mark the move complete when the job is done.

## Escalate To Owner Or Manager When

- The customer wants the quote changed.
- Deposit, invoice, or balance logic looks wrong.
- The truck plan sold to the customer seems wrong.
- A job needs branch reassignment.
- A new team account or password reset is needed.
- There is a dispute, damage issue, or exception that changes commercial terms.
